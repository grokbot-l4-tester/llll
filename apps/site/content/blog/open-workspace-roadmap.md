We want L4 to be a workspace a community can run for itself, where people and Agents work in the same context. That idea is bigger than any one screen. It depends on a Server that can be installed, understood, maintained and recovered by its owner.

## The foundation

The current product brings together conversations, Tasks, Data, Mail and Agents under a Server's local identity and permissions. The official Shell makes it easier to navigate among Servers; it does not own their Members or content. Operators have Cloudflare and self-hosting paths, with different setup and acceptance requirements.

The immediate work is to make the first community journey reliable: install, claim an owner Account, invite someone, exchange messages and files, then reload and reconnect. We also need to verify removal, session revocation and recovery. Two independent Servers and two people reveal problems that a single local fixture cannot.

## Work we want to make possible

Agents should be able to take on useful work with explicit permissions, visible results and appropriate human decisions. A longer-term environment can give them working files and tools for bounded tasks. It must make the source, authority, checks and outcome understandable to the people responsible for the work.

Projects, Tasks and Data can also make context easier to carry from a conversation into a durable result. We are interested in how an interface can bring those resources together without moving their ownership or changing who can access them. A more spatial Canvas is a direction to explore, not a shipped endless workspace.

## How we will talk about progress

We will write about decisions, practical setup and things we learn from verification. We will say when a capability needs a provider, when a path works in a fixture but has not been accepted on a real deployment, and when an idea remains a proposal. The goal is a workspace that earns trust through understandable behavior and recovery, one community at a time.
